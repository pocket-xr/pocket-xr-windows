# Pocket XR for Windows 0.4.12

- **Hands with a camera that faces you** (a webcam, or a laptop's own camera). To open the launcher or recentre, turn
  your palm to your face, as with the glasses' camera; to that camera it is the back of your hand. Before, it wanted
  the palm to the camera, which is what a hand shows it when it pinches at a screen, so pinch and hold could recentre
  instead of grabbing a window. Settings, Hands now says it this way. The same fix is in Pocket XR for Mac 0.5.12,
  where a developer found it with a MacBook's camera.

**Not yet tried:** the change was checked only with the core checks on made-up hands, not with real hands in front of
a webcam. With the glasses' own camera nothing changes.

Everything else is as in 0.4.11.

## Install

1. Download `PocketXR-Windows-0.4.12.zip` and unzip it anywhere (over an older version is fine: your settings are kept).
2. Open `PocketXR.exe`; it closes an older Pocket XR that is still running. It is not signed yet, so Windows may say
   "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
