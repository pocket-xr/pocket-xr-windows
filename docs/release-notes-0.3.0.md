# Pocket XR for Windows 0.3.0

**Steady mode, your way.** Two things a tester asked for while using the XREAL 1S on Windows 11 for their Excel work.

- **Screen gap**: choose the space between your screens in Steady mode, from touching (0°) to wide (3°). It is in the
  control panel ("Gap", under Size, while Steady mode is on) and in Settings, Glasses, Steady mode. It starts where it
  was, at 0.6°.
- **Steady mode is remembered**: if it was on, it comes back on by itself the next time your glasses connect or
  Pocket XR starts. Turning it off is remembered too. To open Pocket XR when you sign in to Windows, see Settings.

Thank you to the tester who reported the **XREAL 1S** working on Windows 11 for hours, in normal and Steady mode: the
first glasses besides the Aura on the Windows version. The app now says so in its list of glasses.

Not yet tried with glasses: bringing Steady mode back on by itself, and the screen gap in the glasses. Both were
checked with the app's own checks only. **Send logs to Mike** in the menu helps, whether it works or not.

## Install

1. Download `PocketXR-Windows-0.3.0.zip` and unzip it anywhere (over an older version is fine: your settings are kept).
2. Open `PocketXR.exe`. It is not signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**,
   then **Run anyway**.

Everything else is as in 0.2.0 (hand tracking) and 0.1.0: see their notes.
