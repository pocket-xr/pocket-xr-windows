# Pocket XR for Windows 0.4.4

- **Taps on the glasses.** Tap the frame twice to recentre, three times for the next layout (the same as
  Ctrl+Alt+L). Pocket XR reads the taps from the glasses' own motion sensor, for every model whose head tracking it
  reads, so they work with no keyboard or controller to hand. It is the Steam Deck's tap reading, with the same
  settings: a short, sharp jolt after a quiet moment, while your head is fairly still, in runs of two or three. Head
  turns and walking are left out.

  Off by default: turn it on in Settings, Head tracking, "Taps on the glasses". Untested with real taps on glasses:
  if you try it, **Send logs to Mike** in the menu helps a lot (each run of taps is logged). Asked for by Pierre
  Zammit.

Everything else is as in 0.4.3.

## Install

1. Download `PocketXR-Windows-0.4.4.zip` and unzip it anywhere (over an older version is fine: your settings are kept).
2. Open `PocketXR.exe`. It is not signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**,
   then **Run anyway**.
